# Home style ideas

Built: **hinge**, **board** and **tape** (see the README). These are the backups, kept for later.
Every one is a physical control you move with your finger; none plays on its own.

1. **Page turn.** The inner screen is an open book: apps on the left page, folders on the right.
   Swipe from the right edge and the next page curls over the crease. The cover shows the spine.
2. **Slide rule.** Two rulers: folders on top, their apps below. Slide the top one and the bottom
   one follows whatever sits under the hairline.
3. **Tuning dial.** A long radio band with apps as stations and a needle you drag. Names fade in
   only as the needle nears them.
4. **Typewriter carriage.** All names on one line. Drag it like a carriage; whatever is under the
   fixed cursor is underlined. Push it to the end and it dings back.
5. **Thread.** Draw a line with a finger; names attach to it like beads as you pass. Lift on the
   one you want.
6. **Sundial.** Apps in a circle around the clock, ordered by time of day. Drag the shadow round to
   see what is next.
7. **Magnet.** Names drift loosely; drag a finger in and nearby names are pulled to it, the
   nearest snapping into the centre.
